Privacy Policy

Stackd · Last updated October 6, 2026

Stackd is a personal budgeting app. We built it so your financial information stays with you. The short version: we don't collect, see, store, or sell any of your data.

Information we collect

None. Stackd does not require an account and does not send your information to us or to anyone else. It has no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

The income, budgets, expenses, bills, savings goals, debts, challenges, streaks, badges, and money personality quiz result you enter or earn are saved only on your device. If your device's backup (such as iCloud Backup) is turned on, this data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Backups you export

If you use "Export backup," the app creates a file and opens your device's share menu. You choose where that file goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive, or email. We never receive a copy.

Sharing your wins

When you tap Share on a win card, Wrapped recap, quiz result, or challenge invite, the image or message is created on your device and handed to your device's share menu. You choose where it goes. Dollar amounts are hidden on win cards unless you turn them on. Stackd never sees or stores what you share.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID lock, your device checks your face or passcode. Stackd only learns whether the check succeeded. It never has access to your biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to a server.

If you email us

If you write to us for support or to join the Android waitlist, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you or to invite you to test Stackd, and we don't share or sell it. Ask us anytime and we'll delete it.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time from More → Erase all data, or by deleting the app.

Children

Stackd is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not financial advice

Stackd is a tool for organizing your own finances. Payoff dates, interest, and other figures are estimates and are not financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
