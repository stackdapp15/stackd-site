Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Stackd works without an account, and your data stays on your iPhone.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

If iCloud Backup is on, your data comes with your phone backup. You can also go to More → Export backup, save the file to iCloud Drive or Files, then use More → Restore from backup on the new phone.

What does "You're good for" mean?

It's how much you can spend per day for the rest of the month, after setting aside unpaid bills and the savings and debt payments you've planned.

How is my debt payoff date calculated?

Stackd simulates your minimum payments plus any extra payment, month by month, using the interest rates you enter. It's an estimate. Your lender's actual interest may differ, so update balances from your statements now and then.

How do streaks work?

Log an expense, a savings deposit, a debt payment, or tap "Check in" on days you don't spend. Each day you do counts toward your streak.

How do I delete my data?

Go to More → Erase all data, or delete the app.
